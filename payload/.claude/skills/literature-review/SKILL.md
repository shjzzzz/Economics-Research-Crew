# Literature Review Workflow
1. Clarify topic, years, languages, inclusion criteria.
2. Search scholarly and official sources; record date and terms.
3. Screen by relevance and source type.
4. Extract question, theory, data, sample, method, identification, findings, limitations.
5. Build a literature matrix; group by mechanism, method, findings or debate.
6. Identify genuine gaps and competing explanations.
7. Draft synthesis with citations and a list of unverified items.

Never invent sources or treat abstracts as full-text evidence.
