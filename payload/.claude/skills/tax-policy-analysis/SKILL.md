# Tax Policy Analysis
1. Define instrument, taxpayer, period, jurisdiction, question.
2. Identify authoritative legal sources; verify effective dates and validity.
3. Separate legal form, actual relationship, statutory liability, remittance, economic incidence.
4. Map behavioral channels: reporting, avoidance, evasion, labor supply, investment, organizational form, pass-through.
5. Assess efficiency, equity, administrative cost, compliance, distribution.
6. Note implementation constraints and competing interpretations.
7. Provide a source table; flag conclusions needing official confirmation.

Never infer legal classification from a platform's contract label alone.
